### Indian Military Phrases

#### Greetings & Protocols
- **"Jai Hind!"** - Victory to India (salutation)
- **"Bharat Mata Ki Jai!"** - Victory to Mother India
- **"Sena Nayak!"** - Army Leader
- **"Vande Mataram"** - I bow to thee, Mother

#### Common Military Terms
- **Sahab/Sahib** - Sir (respectful address)
- **Jawan** - Soldier (literally "young man")
- **Sainik** - Soldier
- **Paltan** - Regiment/Battalion
- **Cantt** - Cantonment (military station)
- **Fauj** - Army (colloquial)
- **Shahadat** - Martyrdom
- **Param Vir Chakra** - India's highest military decoration
- **Vir Chakra** - Gallantry award
- **Shaurya Chakra** - Bravery award

#### Operational Terms
- **Op Sector** - Operational Sector
- **ORBAT** - Order of Battle
- **RV** - Rendezvous
- **FUP** - Forming Up Place
- **LD** - Line of Departure
- **LOC** - Line of Control
- **Recce** - Reconnaissance
- **Sitrep** - Situation Report
- **CASEVAC** - Casualty Evacuation
- **Fire Assay** - Fire Support
- **Stand-To** - Alert state
- **Stand-Down** - Relaxed state

### Rank Progression System

Instead of "Level 85", use:

**Proficiency Ranks:**
- **0-20**: Recruit (भर्ती)
- **21-40**: Sepoy (सिपाही)
- **41-60**: Lance Naik (लांस नायक)
- **61-75**: Naik (नायक)
- **76-85**: Havildar (हवलदार)
- **86-95**: Junior Commissioned Officer (JCO)
- **96-100**: Commissioned Officer

For our levels:
- Frontend: 85 = **Havildar Rank** ⭐⭐⭐
